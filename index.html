My story

Amani was created with a simple intention: to share food that brings comfort, connection, and a sense of peace.

My story is shaped by late night chai conversations, family kitchens filled with the scent of cardamom and butter, and recipes passed down, reimagined, and shared with love. What started as a small dessert concept centered around my signature flavored milk cakes and savory, flaky bites has grown into something more. It is now where cultures meet, where tradition feels modern, and where every detail is intentional.

Each milk cake is crafted to be soft, indulgent, and deeply nostalgic. Each savory bite is layered, buttery, and made to be shared. Every cup of coffee is an invitation to slow down.

Amani means peace, and that is what I hope you feel in every bite and every sip. I'd love to share with you what I love most: food that feels like care and experiences that leave you just a little more at peace than when you arrived.
